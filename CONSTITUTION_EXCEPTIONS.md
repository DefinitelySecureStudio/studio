# Constitution exception register

This file is the authoritative public index of temporary constitutional
exceptions governed by
[Article 12 of the Constitution](CONSTITUTION.md#127-temporary-constitutional-exceptions).
An entry is not valid merely because it appears here: it also requires the exact
A4 approvals, eligibility, evidence, scope, and unexpired term required by the
Constitution.

Sensitive evidence belongs in a protected system. The public entry must retain
a reader-safe summary and a random, non-derivable attestation identifier that an
authorized reviewer can resolve to the complete restricted record. This register
never contains secrets, personal data, private Lore, live vulnerability details,
or questionable rights material.

## Active exceptions

None.

## Historical exceptions

None.

## Required entry fields

Each active or historical entry records:

- stable exception identifier and status;
- Constitution version, immutable revision, article, and exact eligible
  requirement;
- accountable owner, affected A4 domain owners, constitutional steward, and
  approval times;
- exact scope covering systems, actions, data, artifacts, audience, destination,
  repositories, and prohibited uses;
- necessity, alternatives, rationale, risks, compensating controls, monitoring,
  stop conditions, rollback, remediation, and notification;
- start, expiry of no more than 90 days, renewal lineage, and review schedule;
- public evidence and a safe restricted-record attestation when needed;
- actual use, affected outputs and downstream consumers, incidents, residual
  obligations, validation, and closure outcome; and
- linked issues, pull requests, amendments, policies, and follow-up work.

Denied, revoked, expired, superseded, and closed entries remain in the historical
section. Renewal creates a new linked entry and never edits an expired term into
an active one.
