# Definitely Secure Studio identity

Status: Proposed

Owner: Definitely Secure Studio

Last updated: 2026-08-11

## Core identity

- **Official studio name:** Definitely Secure Studio
- **Short-form studio name:** the Studio (running prose only, after the full name)
- **Public-facing display name:** Definitely Secure Studio
- **Flagship creative property:** *Definitely Secure*
- **Tagline:** Serious craft. Questionable systems.
- **Primary domain:** `definitelysecure.com`
- **GitHub organization:** `DefinitelySecureStudio`

Definitely Secure Studio is an independent creative studio making character-driven stories, publications, animation, and open-source tools about technology, work, and the people caught inside both.

### Positioning statement

For readers and makers who recognize the absurdity inside modern systems, Definitely Secure Studio creates sharply observed, character-driven worlds across comics, animation, publishing, and software. Unlike a single-title comics imprint or a fictional technology brand, the Studio is the real-world creative organization that develops the *Definitely Secure* universe and future work with the same combination of humor, empathy, and technical curiosity.

### Mission statement

Definitely Secure Studio creates funny, humane, technically curious stories and tools that help people see the systems shaping their work and lives—and imagine better ones.

## Brand architecture

```text
Definitely Secure Studio (real-world studio and publisher)
├── Definitely Secure (flagship comic and creative universe)
│   ├── Definitely Secure (fictional workplace/company)
│   ├── Characters and lore
│   └── Supporting stories and publications
├── Future creative properties
└── Open-source tools and experiments
```

The same name intentionally identifies the flagship title and its fictional workplace. Typography and context distinguish them: italicize *Definitely Secure* when referring to the comic or creative work; use plain text, optionally followed by “the fictional company,” when referring to the organization inside the story.

Definitely Secure Studio is the real-world creator and publisher. It does not exist inside the fictional universe unless a specific work introduces it as an explicit, clearly signposted meta-fictional device.

Open-source tools belong to Definitely Secure Studio unless a tool develops a distinct product brand. A product name may lead on its own surfaces, but its documentation and repository retain Studio attribution. There is no separate engineering identity.

## Approved public language

### One-sentence description

Definitely Secure Studio creates character-driven comics, animation, publications, and open-source tools about technology, work, and the people caught inside both.

### Short biography

Definitely Secure Studio is an independent creative studio exploring technology, work, and modern systems through character-driven comics, animation, publications, and open-source tools. It is the home of *Definitely Secure*, a workplace comic where technical confidence and operational reality rarely agree. Serious craft. Questionable systems—for curious readers, artists, and builders.

### Long biography

Definitely Secure Studio is an independent creative studio making character-driven stories about technology, work, and the systems people build, inherit, and struggle to understand. The Studio develops comics, publications, animation, creative tools, and new fictional worlds with a voice that pairs technical curiosity with sharp humor and genuine affection for the people inside the machine.

Its flagship property, *Definitely Secure*, is a workplace comic and expanding creative universe centered on a fictional company where every system is under control—at least according to the status report. Through its characters and lore, the series explores the distance between confident language and messy reality without losing sight of the humans doing their best in between.

Definitely Secure Studio also builds open-source tools and experiments that support its own creative practice and help other makers publish, collaborate, and tell stories. Those tools share the Studio’s values, but they are not fictional artifacts and do not require knowledge of the comic. This structure leaves room for future titles and formats while keeping a clear promise across everything the Studio makes: serious craft, questionable systems, and work that rewards a closer look.

### Credits and attribution

**Production credit**

> A Definitely Secure Studio production.

For work jointly produced with another organization, use “Produced by Definitely Secure Studio and [Partner]” or a negotiated equivalent instead of implying sole production.

**Copyright line**

> © [YEAR] Definitely Secure Studio. All rights reserved.

Use a year range for work published across multiple years. This brand wording does not replace the registered legal name if law or a contract requires one.

**Repository attribution**

> An open-source project from Definitely Secure Studio. See the repository license for terms.

Place this line in repository documentation where Studio ownership is useful. The applicable open-source license controls reuse; do not append “All rights reserved” in a way that contradicts it.

## Channel presentation

### Website

The global site header and footer use “Definitely Secure Studio.” A page dedicated to the flagship title may lead with “Definitely Secure,” provided the Studio name remains visible in the header, footer, metadata, or production credit. The root domain represents the Studio and may host the flagship property alongside future work.

### Social media

Use “Definitely Secure Studio” as the display name. Prefer `@definitelysecure` for the handle when available; use `@definitelysecurestudio` when clarity or availability requires it. Account biographies identify the Studio as the home of *Definitely Secure*. Do not present the account as though the fictional company operates it unless a campaign is clearly labeled as in-world storytelling.

### Repositories

Use `DefinitelySecureStudio` exactly for the GitHub organization. Repository prose uses “Definitely Secure Studio.” Individual tools can have independent names, but their README, package metadata where appropriate, and documentation should name the Studio as creator or maintainer.

## Naming rules

| Do | Don’t | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Definitely Secure Studio | DefinitelySecure Studio | The studio name is three spaced words. |
| Definitely Secure Studio | Definitely Secure Studios | “Studio” is singular. |
| Definitely Secure Studio | Def Secure Studio | Do not abbreviate the public name. |
| the Studio | DSS | “The Studio” is the only approved prose shorthand, after first use. |
| *Definitely Secure* (the comic) | Definitely Secure Inc. (the comic) | The official title has no corporate suffix. |
| Definitely Secure (the fictional company) | Definitely Secure Studio (the fictional company) | Keep the real studio outside the story world. |
| DefinitelySecureStudio (GitHub only) | definitelysecurestudio in prose | The unspaced form is a technical identifier, not a display name. |
| definitelysecure.com | DefinitelySecure.com | Domains are lowercase in styled text. |
| A Definitely Secure Studio production. | Presented by Definitely Secure. | Credits identify the real producer, not the fictional brand. |

### Capitalization and spacing

- Capitalize all three words in “Definitely Secure Studio.”
- Always put spaces between the words except in technical identifiers that do not permit them.
- Use title case for the Studio and property names in all languages that follow English brand styling.
- Use the lowercase article in “the Studio” unless it begins a sentence.
- Preserve the exact casing of registered handles and technical identifiers where the platform exposes it.

### Abbreviations and suffixes

“The Studio” is acceptable only after “Definitely Secure Studio” has appeared in the same document or page. `DSS` may be used privately as a compact internal identifier, never as a public display name or standalone attribution.

Do not add “Inc.,” “LLC,” “Ltd.,” “Press,” “Comics,” “Productions,” or “Studios” to the brand name. A legal entity suffix may appear in contracts, invoices, tax documents, copyright ownership records, or other contexts that require the entity’s registered legal name; it does not become part of the public brand. “Press,” “Comics,” or another imprint may be proposed later as a separately named sub-brand, not used casually as a variation of the Studio name.

## Editorial test

Before publishing, ask:

1. Does the language distinguish the real Studio from the *Definitely Secure* property and fictional company?
2. Is the full studio name visible at least once?
3. Is the unspaced organization name limited to a technical identifier?
4. Does the credit describe the Studio’s actual role without overstating ownership or production?
5. If the material is open source, does the license—not the brand copy—define reuse rights?
