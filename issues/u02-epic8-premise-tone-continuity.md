## Premise

### Sourced series promise

The approved Studio identity describes *Definitely Secure* as a workplace comic about confident systems, messy reality, and people trying to keep both running. Its humor is technically observant and character-driven, and should work for readers without specialist knowledge. The comic's premise lives in the gap between how work is described and how it actually happens. See the pinned [comic identity](https://github.com/DefinitelySecureStudio/studio/blob/9a3e9dec66f1aa46fefc39b6b7c6944e7cf755a8/brand/comic-identity.md) and its [approval record](https://github.com/DefinitelySecureStudio/studio/blob/9a3e9dec66f1aa46fefc39b6b7c6944e7cf755a8/brand/APPROVAL.md).

### Accepted seed for exploration

At one shared workplace inside fictional Definitely Secure, a routine status report says the work is complete while observable work remains unresolved. The report's owner and one coworker who checks evidence compare what they can verify, then agree on a truthful next update.

The comic's engine is the small, recognizable distance between a confident status and the work in front of the coworkers. The cause, the report format, and what counts as evidence remain open. This seed does not say anyone lied, define a technical failure, or claim a real event happened.

## Tone rules proposed for this seed

- Let the status/work mismatch carry the joke. Keep the technical detail understandable from the coworkers' actions and plain-language context.
- Aim the humor at overconfident wording, process friction, and the gap between a label and reality. Keep both coworkers capable and human; neither is the punchline for missing information.
- Prefer a precise, understated turn over jargon, panic, a lecture, or an incident-postmortem voice. A reader should understand the human problem without knowing how a particular system works.
- Leave room for the coworkers to compare evidence and cooperate. Their truthful update is a small act of care and coordination, not a grand technical fix.

These are new U02 proposals grounded in the approved reader promise; they are not additional approved brand rules or character traits.

## Setting rules proposed for this seed

- The company is the fictional Definitely Secure. Keep it distinct from Definitely Secure Studio, the real-world producer.
- Keep the action within one shared workplace. Its name, physical or virtual form, geography, layout, and visual design are undecided and belong with the U03 location decision.
- A report, dashboard, policy, or other ordinary workplace artifact may make the mismatch visible. No specific tool, security architecture, company policy, team structure, or technical behavior is established here.
- Show only enough of the setting to make the report and the remaining work legible. Do not add a second site, an off-screen coworker, or a larger organization to explain the joke.

These constraints narrow the accepted seed; they do not establish a general company operating model.

## Continuity conventions

### Sourced series conventions

The approved comic identity says individual strips stand alone while recurring characters, workplace history, and consequences build over time. Published episode numbers follow publication order, not in-story chronology; the public identifier uses `DS-NNNN`. This proposal assigns no number, title, or chronology. See the pinned [comic identity](https://github.com/DefinitelySecureStudio/studio/blob/9a3e9dec66f1aa46fefc39b6b7c6944e7cf755a8/brand/comic-identity.md).

### Working conventions for this proposed seed

- A reader can follow this situation without knowing earlier stories. Do not imply a previous incident, ongoing plot, or established history for either coworker.
- Keep the relationship at the accepted coworker level. This sheet adds no reporting hierarchy, friendship history, or other relationship.
- Let the report's claim, what the checker can observe, and the coworkers' interpretation remain distinguishable. Do not turn one into proof of a cause the editor has not selected.
- End this seed at agreement on a truthful next update. Whether the work is later finished, what consequences follow, and whether this workplace or characters recur remain open for human editorial choice.
- A draft or repeated detail does not become canon by itself. Studio tracks this proposal; reader-safe story facts belong in Universe only after the applicable human editorial decision. See [Studio's repository authority map](https://github.com/DefinitelySecureStudio/studio/blob/9a3e9dec66f1aa46fefc39b6b7c6944e7cf755a8/ARCHITECTURE.md) and the [Universe canon policy](https://github.com/DefinitelySecureStudio/universe/blob/66bf9b0d24329a134808b39e1d8898d9bd87722c/docs/canon-policy.md) pinned by U01.
